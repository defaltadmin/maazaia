A Saudi manpower company, built around the paperwork nobody else wants.

Maazaia supplies contract labour, recruits permanent staff and administers the employment behind both, for clients across the Kingdom.

Background

Maazaia was established in [YEAR] in Riyadh to solve a narrow, expensive problem: employers in the Kingdom could find workers, but they could not reliably get them hired, sponsored, paid and supervised without the process becoming a second job.

We began with construction labour supply in the Central Region, then extended into facilities management, industrial and hospitality staffing as clients asked us to take on more of the employment chain. Today we operate from Riyadh with representation in Jeddah and Dammam, recruit from nine overseas markets, and employ workers directly on Maazaia sponsorship as well as recruiting onto client sponsorship.

We are deliberately not a CV-forwarding agency. Every service we sell ends in an obligation we carry: a worker on a site, on our payroll, with a valid iqama and a named supervisor.

How we work
01

Compliance is the product

Ajeer contracts, WPS payroll, GOSI registration and iqama validity are not add-ons we remember at audit time. They are the reason clients outsource to us.

02

No candidate fees

Workers never pay us, at any stage, in any country. Our overseas partners are contractually bound to the same rule and audited on it.

03

Ethical recruitment

Written contracts in a language the worker reads, accommodation that meets Kingdom standards, passports retained by the worker, and salaries paid on time through the banking system.

04

Named accountability

Every contract has a Maazaia account manager and, on site, a supervisor. Escalation does not mean emailing a shared inbox.
