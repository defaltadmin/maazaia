Apply for jobs

One application puts you in front of employers across the Kingdom. We keep your file active for twelve months and contact you when a role matches.

We never charge candidates

Maazaia does not charge jobseekers any fee, deposit or commission, in the Kingdom or in your home country, at any stage. Our client employers pay us. If anyone asks you for money using our name, stop and report it to us.

Before you submit
  • Attach a CV as PDF, DOC or DOCX, under 5 MB.
  • If you are inside the Kingdom, state whether your iqama is transferable.
  • Include certificates and trade test results in the same file if you have them.
  • Applications are reviewed in the order received; we reply only when there is a match.
