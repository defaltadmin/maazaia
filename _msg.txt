feat: fix CSP console error, add Arabic legal pages, dark mode, animations

Reported issues:
- /ar/privacy 404 (no Arabic legal pages existed)
- /cookies was English-only
- "Send your CV" unreadable on the hero
- CSP blocked Cloudflare's beacon, logging a console error
- Best Practices stuck at 92
- wanted more animation, an animated background and dark mode

Fixes:
- CSP: allow static.cloudflareinsights.com in script-src and connect-src.
  Cloudflare Web Analytics was being blocked by our own policy; that was
  the entire Best Practices deduction, plus the console error.
- Added ar/privacy, ar/terms and ar/cookies. Each is a real Arabic
  document, not a stub, and states plainly that the English text is the
  controlling legal version where the two differ.
- Language switchers now land on the matching document in the other
  language instead of that language's homepage.
- "Send your CV" used .btn--ghost, which sets a dark ink label intended
  for light surfaces. On the dark hero it rendered near-black. Added
  .btn--ondark for use on dark backgrounds and swapped both instances.
- Fonts no longer block first paint: the Google stylesheet is preloaded
  and switched to media="print"/onload, with a noscript fallback.
  FCP 4.1s -> 0.9s, LCP 4.1s -> 1.2s.

Dark mode:
- Manual toggle plus follows the OS preference until a choice is made
- Applied by a tiny inline script before first paint, so there is no
  flash of the wrong theme
- Separate token block; the brand button fill needed its own token
  because the lighter teal only reaches 3.3:1 behind white text
- Header wordmark re-pointed at --ink, since --brand-deep resolves to
  near-black on the dark surface and was invisible

Animation:
- Slow drifting ambient background, clipped with contain:strict so it
  cannot affect layout (as a flow child it inflated <main> and caused
  a 0.196 CLS on the Arabic page)
- Gradient sweep on the primary CTA, growing underlines in the footer,
  lift on cards and pills
- All of it freezes under prefers-reduced-motion

Verified locally with Lighthouse: English 100/100/100/100, Arabic
99/100/100/100 with CLS 0.069, zero console errors. A metric-matched
font fallback was added to reduce Arabic reflow; values are conservative
because over-scaling the fallback shifts text further than the swap.
