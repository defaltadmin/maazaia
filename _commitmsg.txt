The site was publishing a fair amount of invented information. All of it is
now gone; only claims that can actually be defended remain.

Removed (was fabricated, never confirmed):
- phone and WhatsApp numbers, postal address, map coordinates
- Riyadh / Jeddah / Dammam office claims
- MHRSD licence and commercial registration numbers
- the "Licensed by MHRSD" and "WPS-compliant" claims
- invented statistics (4,000+ workers, 96% renewal, 21-day mobilisation)
- leadership names and the company origin narrative
- unconfirmed sales@, careers@ and privacy@ mailboxes
- assumed business hours

Contact is now email only: info@maazaia.com, via mailto on every CTA.
JSON-LD keeps EmploymentAgency but drops telephone/address/geo/
openingHours, so no unverified identity is published to search engines.

Fixes an Arabic readability bug:
- the trust strip was rendering outside the hero section, so its light text
  landed on the light band background
- rebuilt ar/index.html with the same section structure as the English page
- dark sections now re-declare their own text tokens, so light-on-light
  cannot recur in either text direction

Cookie policy:
- new /cookies page stating plainly that no tracking cookies, analytics or ad
  tech are used
- a notice on both homepages records the choice in local storage only; it is
  informational rather than a consent gate because there is nothing to
  consent to yet

Design:

- sticky blurred header that gains a shadow on scroll
- restrained scroll-reveal animations, disabled under reduced-motion
- hero with layered gradients and a fine dot texture
- darker accent shades so every text pair clears WCAG AA
- all 22 colour pairs verified; the eyebrow and primary CTA were failing at
  3.6:1 and 4.0:1 before this change
