1. Who we are

Maazaia is a manpower and recruitment business in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We operate maazaia.com and are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

This policy covers this website only. It does not describe how we handle information in our recruitment, payroll or client work, which is governed by our agreements and by Saudi law.

We handle personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations (the PDPL).

2. What we collect

What you send us. When you use the contact form we receive your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message.

Technical data. Our hosting provider receives your IP address and standard request details (such as browser type and the page requested) in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Please do not put CVs, identity document details, bank details or other sensitive personal data into the contact form. The form has no file upload.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers.

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the name mz-theme. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

The site serves its own fonts and makes no requests to third-party font services.

4. How we use it

We use what you send through the contact form to read and respond to your enquiry. We use technical data to keep the site and the form secure and working.

Our basis for this is your request to hear from us and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and securing the website. We do not sell personal data and we do not use contact-form messages for marketing.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the site, acting on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare, to host, deliver and protect the website, including any security check shown on the contact form.
  • An email delivery provider, which passes the contact-form message to our inbox.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it or to establish or defend legal rights.

6. Transfers outside the Kingdom

These providers operate internationally, so contact-form messages and technical data may be processed outside Saudi Arabia. Where that happens we rely on the transfer mechanisms the PDPL allows.

7. How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages for as long as we need them to respond to your enquiry and to keep ordinary business records of it, then we delete them. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for its standard operational period.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have rights over your personal data, including to be informed, to access it, to ask for it to be corrected or destroyed, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise a right, use the contact form and choose “Other enquiry”.

You may also complain to the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), the PDPL supervisory authority.

9. Security

The website and the contact form are served over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the new version on this page with a new date. For any privacy question, use the contact form and choose “Other enquiry”.
