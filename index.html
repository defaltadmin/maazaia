Who we are

A staffing partner built for compliance-heavy work.

Manpower in the Kingdom is not a headcount problem. It is a compliance problem with a headcount attached. Visas, Saudization bands, WPS payroll, GOSI, iqama renewals, accommodation standards — any one of them can stall a project that is otherwise ready to run.

Maazaia takes the whole chain. We recruit locally and from overseas markets, process the government paperwork ourselves, employ the workers on our own sponsorship where you need it, and keep supervisors on site so productivity is somebody's job rather than everybody's hope.
