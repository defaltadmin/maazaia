What we do

Four services, one point of contact.

Take one service or all four. Most engagements start with manpower supply and add compliance support once the first crew mobilises.

01

Contract manpower

Trades, technical and industrial crews supplied on contract terms, mobilised and supervised by our own staff.

  • Trades, HVAC, electrical, mechanical
  • Industrial and warehouse teams
  • Hospitality and facilities
02

Permanent recruitment

Local and overseas hires for roles you need filled permanently, including Saudization-ready candidates.

  • Local talent sourcing
  • Overseas recruitment drives
  • Offer negotiation and onboarding
03

Visa & iqama processing

The government paperwork handled end to end, so your schedule does not depend on someone else's queue.

  • Work permits and visa processing
  • iqama issuance and renewal
  • MHRSD and GOSI registrations
04

Payroll & supervision

Accurate payroll and on-site supervision, so output is somebody's job rather than everybody's hope.

  • Payroll processing
  • GOSI and end-of-service handling
  • Site supervisors and reporting

How it works

From first email to crew on site.

Send the scope

Trades, headcount, location and start date. A short email is enough to begin.

Step 02

Availability and indicative rates, with no obligation attached.

Supervision and payroll

Crews report to our supervisors. Payroll runs on a fixed monthly cycle.
