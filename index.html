About Maazaia

A staffing partner built for compliance-heavy work.

Manpower in the Kingdom is not a headcount problem, it is a compliance problem with a headcount attached. Visas, Saudization bands, WPS payroll, GOSI, iqama renewals, accommodation standards: any one of them can stall a project that is otherwise ready to run.

Maazaia takes the whole chain. We recruit locally and from nine overseas markets, process the government paperwork ourselves, employ the workers on our own sponsorship where you need it, and keep supervisors on site so productivity is somebody's job rather than everybody's hope.

Questions

What employers and candidates ask first.

Do you charge jobseekers any fees?

No. Saudi regulation prohibits charging candidates recruitment fees, and we do not charge them under any label. Employers pay our fees. If anyone requests money in Maazaia's name, report it to us immediately.

Who is the legal employer of outsourced workers?

For manpower outsourcing, Maazaia is the employer of record. We hold the sponsorship, pay salaries through WPS, and carry GOSI and end-of-service obligations. You direct the day-to-day work through a service agreement.

How quickly can you mobilize?

Workers already in the Kingdom with transferable iqamas can start within days. Overseas recruitment normally runs four to ten weeks depending on source country, visa availability and medical clearance.

Can you recruit onto our own visas instead?

Yes. We can source, screen and process candidates under your block visas and sponsorship, and charge a per-hire recruitment fee instead of a monthly supply rate.

Which cities do you cover?

The whole Kingdom. Offices in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam, and we mobilize to remote project sites with accommodation, transport and supervision arranged.

How is our data and candidate data handled?

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. The privacy policy sets out the legal bases we rely on, retention periods, transfers outside the Kingdom and how to exercise your rights.
