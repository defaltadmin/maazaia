Cookie policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This policy explains what maazaia.com stores in your browser. The short version: this site does not set tracking cookies and does not run analytics.

1. What we store

When you dismiss the cookie notice, we save your choice in your browser's local storage under the key mz-cookie-choice. This is:

  • readable only by this site, on this device;
  • never transmitted to us or to any third party;
  • used only to avoid showing the notice again.

Local storage is not a cookie and is not sent with requests to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes it, after which the notice will appear again.

2. Cookies set by the site

None. Maazaia sets no first-party cookies of its own.

3. Third-party requests

Our stylesheet is served from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com when a page loads. Google may log that request in the ordinary course of operating its service. We do not send you any identifier and we do not use that request for profiling.

We do not embed advertising networks, analytics scripts, social trackers, chat widgets, video players, or map services.

4. Server logs

Cloudflare, which hosts this site, processes network requests in order to serve pages and to protect against abuse. This is a security and delivery function, not behavioural tracking, and we do not use it to build a profile of visitors.

5. Email and enquiries

If you email us, the contents of your message and your email address are processed so we can reply. That is a separate matter from cookies and is covered in our privacy policy.

6. If this changes

If we later introduce analytics or any non-essential technology, we will update this page, adjust the notice, and ask for consent before setting anything non-essential.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@maazaia.com
