fix: repair font <link> href broken by a bad regex substitution

The previous pass built the replacement string with str.format(), which
does not expand regex backreferences. The literal text \1 was written into
every page as the stylesheet href, so the browser requested
https://maazaia.com/1 three times and refused it as an unsupported MIME
type. That was the remaining Best Practices deduction (96, not 100).

All nine pages now carry the real fonts.googleapis.com URL in the
preload, the async stylesheet link and the noscript fallback.
