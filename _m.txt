perf: split the no-flash theme script out of main.js

Loading all of main.js synchronously in <head> to avoid a flash of the
wrong theme cost First Contentful Paint 0.9s -> 4.1s, because a
render-blocking script delays the first paint by a full round trip.

The theme code moved to assets/js/theme.js (841 bytes) which is preloaded
and loaded synchronously. That is small enough to be negligible, and it
still runs before the body is parsed so the flash does not come back.
main.js stays deferred and keeps everything else.

Also de-duplicated the deferred main.js tag on 404, privacy and terms,
which ended up with two after the split.

Verified: Lighthouse 100/100/100/100, zero console errors.
